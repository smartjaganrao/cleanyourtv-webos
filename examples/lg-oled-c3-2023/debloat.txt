# LG OLED C3 Recommended Debloat & Optimization Checklist
1. Settings → General → System → Additional Settings → Home Settings:
   - Home Auto Launch = OFF (Prevents intrusive LG webOS full-screen home dashboard)
   - Home Promotion = OFF (Disables sponsored trending carousels)
2. Settings → General → OLED Care:
   - Pixel Cleaning = Auto
   - Screen Move (Pixel Shift) = ON (CleanHub TV Art Mode includes companion burn-in prevention)
3. Settings → General → System → Additional Settings:
   - Live Plus (ACR Tracking) = OFF
   - Advertisement / Limit AD Tracking = ON
