# LG 60UP7750PTZ (webOS 6.x) Recommended Settings
1. Settings → General → Additional Settings → Home Settings:
   - Home Auto Launch = OFF
2. Settings → General → Additional Settings:
   - Live Plus = OFF
   - Do Not Sell My Info / Limit AD Tracking = ON
3. Developer Mode:
   - Developer Mode session token renewal can be automated with com.pirate.refresh or cron job.
